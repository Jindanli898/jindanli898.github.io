About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Cornell University, advised by Prof. Tianyi Chen. I received my B.Eng. in Information Engineering from Zhejiang University in July 2024.

Research

My research interests include physical neural networks and analog learning systems, robust training and parameter-efficient LLM adaptation on non-ideal hardware, and reinforcement learning for wireless network optimization.
